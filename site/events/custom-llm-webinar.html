Webinar

From Zero to Your Own ChatGPT

A two-part online webinar on custom language models with Thousand Miles AI. The first session explored what happens inside an LLM. The second moved from prompts to a working application.

Session details

Dates
2 and 9 May 2026
Time
12 noon IST · one hour per session
Format
Online · free registration

What the session covered

2 May · Under the hood

How LLMs predict, how tokenization and context length shape their input, and an agentic AI demonstration.

9 May · Now you drive

Few-shot and chain-of-thought prompting, the roles of system and user prompts, and building a working app live.
